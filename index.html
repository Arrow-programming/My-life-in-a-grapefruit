About me

During covid I was introduced to Khan Academy where I first learned how to code more than the simple shapes I had been doing. I got quite competent with JS but after Covid my interest died down a bit only to be rekindled when I started to use my ability as a programmer to complete school assignments (making custom graphics with KAPJS, etc). About 2 years later a friend encouraged me to try webdesign, and so I learned HTML and CSS by going through the w3schools course and writing everything down, even if I didn't grasp it. My interest was further kindled when I was elected to be a leader for a annual online coding competition held by the Khan Academy community based on my webdesign skills. I have been involved in the coding community ever since, creating projects and contributing to the community in various ways. Last year, I was very happy to be able to lead a team to victory in a differant coding competition. Coding isn't the only thing I do though, I am also somewhat competent at photography, especially focusing on life. I enjoy birding, contributing to various science platforms such as iNaturalist, eBird, etc. I also enjoy chess, reading, art, and basketball.
